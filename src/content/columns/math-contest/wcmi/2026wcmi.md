This is an edited English translation of the 2026 中国西部数学邀请赛 paper. The original paper consists of four problems on each of two days.

## Day 1

### Problem 1

Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_n$ be positive real numbers such that, for every $1\le i\le n$,

$$
\frac{1}{a_i}-\frac{1}{b_i}\le 1.
$$

Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n}
-\frac{1}{b_1+b_2+\cdots+b_n}
\le 1.
$$

### Problem 2

Let $l$ be the tangent at a point $A$ on a circle $\omega$. Choose points $A'$ and $A''$ on $l$ so that they are symmetric about $A$.

A secant through $A'$ meets $\omega$ at $P$ and $Q$, and a secant through $A''$ meets $\omega$ at $S$ and $T$. The lines through $A$ parallel to $PQ$ and $ST$ meet $\omega$ again at $X$ and $Y$, respectively. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $QS$.

Prove that the three lines $AM$, $QY$, and $SX$ are concurrent.

### Problem 3

Let $S=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$, and let $f:S\to S$ be a bijection with the following property: for all $a,b\in S$,

$$
a\mid b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad f(a)\mid f(b).
$$

Prove that if every prime divisor of $k\in S$ is less than $\sqrt n$, then

$$
f(k)=k.
$$

### Problem 4

Let $n$ be a positive even integer. Suppose that real numbers $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ satisfy

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^{n}|x_i|=1.
$$

For $1\le i,j\le n$, define

$$
D_{ij}
=\max\{x_i,x_{i+1},\ldots,x_{i+j-1}\}
-\min\{x_i,x_{i+1},\ldots,x_{i+j-1}\},
$$

where subscripts are interpreted modulo $n$. Find the minimum possible value of

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}D_{ij}.
$$

## Day 2

### Problem 5

Let $H$ be the orthocenter of an acute triangle $ABC$, and let $BD$ and $CE$ be altitudes, with $D\in AC$ and $E\in AB$. A point $P$ inside $ABC$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $BHC$. The line $BP$ meets $AC$ at $Q$, and the line $CP$ meets $AB$ at $R$.

Prove that the line $DE$ passes through the midpoint of $QR$.

### Problem 6

Complex numbers $z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4$ satisfy

$$
z_1+z_2+z_3+z_4=0
$$

and

$$
|z_i-\overline{z_j}|\le 1
\qquad (1\le i,j\le 4).
$$

Find the maximum possible value of

$$
\left|\sum_{1\le i<j\le 4}z_i z_j\right|.
$$

### Problem 7

Let $n$ be a positive integer. On an infinite square grid, $n^2$ cells are selected so that no two selected cells share a vertex.

Among the unselected cells, find the minimum possible number of cells that share at least one vertex with a selected cell.

### Problem 8

Find the smallest positive integer $m$ for which there exists a set $F$ of $m$ ordered triples $(i,j,k)$ satisfying both of the following conditions:

1. For every $(i,j,k)\in F$, the indices $i,j,k$ are pairwise distinct elements of $\{1,2,\ldots,100\}$.
2. For any positive integers $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{100}$, if

   $$
   a_i\mid a_j+a_k
   $$

   for every $(i,j,k)\in F$, then at least $99$ of the numbers $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_{100}$ are equal.
