## Western China Mathematics Invitational

The **Western China Mathematics Invitational** (中国西部数学邀请赛) is an olympiad-style competition for secondary-school students in China. Its papers feature proof-based problems in algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory.

This section collects English translations of the competition papers, together with solution notes and observations about useful techniques.
