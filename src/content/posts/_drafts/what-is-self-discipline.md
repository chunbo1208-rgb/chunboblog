# My comprehension towards self-discipline
## Self-discipline is another form of self-awareness.
For example, if we take the converse situation, somebody doing anything not from himself, but controlled by others. What he does everyday is just finishing reserved tasks, limitlessly.

We can’t claim that such man is a self-disciplined one, but we can only say that he is under an intensive workload.

So that hypothesis shows us that self-discipline is fully self-control and absolutely self-awareness.

But it seems only self-awareness is not enough, we need something deeper.
## Doing right choices and form consistent habits

As long as we all have the ability and awareness to make the right choice every time. Then theoretically, we can save our time and increase to the highest efficiency.

But the only variable is that our energy should be focused on the important tasks, rather than useless choices. So we naturally form habits. Somehow, there are some good habits, like brushing teeth everyday, keeping a diary everyday. There are also some bad habits, like scrolling Tiktok when eating. Before we are clear about which cycle is a bad habit, we form the bad ones more easily.

So actually, our main theme is turned from making right choices to forming good habits and cut off bad habits
# Why we need self-discipline?

Elon musk ever said in a report:
> Everyday we need some reason to wake up.

I think so.

Actually, self-discipline is one of the reasons. 
People have ambitious ideas, clear goals, then they will obsesse and keep the motivation forward.

But we don't always have such attractive ideas and motivated goals. Then how can we keep consistency even in confusion, I think the answer is self-discipline.


# Make it executable

As we all agree the mind game I have stated before, we need to make it happened to be in our everyday life.

Actually, self-discipline never stands for anything, I think a better word should be self-controllable, you control your life, that is important.

Actually, first of all, we need to break our bad cycle, and before all, we have to learn how to **tell** bad cycles.

**Bad** is too emotional to define our habits. Actually, we can apply the standard by whether it is controllable. If not, then it is what we say as **bad**.

For example, scrolling when eating is such one. When you give your brain the signal you will eat, your phone is magically sticked in your hand, and open tiktok or Instagram or whatever news shorts you like, start mindlessly scrolling.

Focus on that, we need to first encounter us outside of the system, like
1. how much it is bonded with the behavior of eating
2. how hard it is to get abide by this

As you start to think questions like that, you have already been aware of this bad cycle, and also outside the cycle itself.

Absolutely, at initial, you will find it hard to get abide by this, and that is natural. You spend days forming, months strengthening, how can you cut it off in just one day? But start stepping is already a great leap, you have wined yourself.
