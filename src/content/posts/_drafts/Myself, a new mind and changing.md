# About myself
## Be really devoted
I'm not joking though, but sincerely, there is nothing in my world out of mathematics. I think somehow, this is the only way in mental area I can do to avoid noises.

As a student, inevitably I need to solve many things, if I see myself as someone who make it best to solve everything: that is the main fault(currently considered) and reason I'm lost.

This is previously my logic chain:

> My ideas won't be accepted by much - I waste energy debate with them - I need to be silent and don't care them - I'm out of track.

Actually, that is the way out I think to keep my "freedom". It is not wrong, but wrong with a mistake and bad preview. We can't fight with the environment, what we do is to make the environment best for us. 

Request the resource we can use as much as we can, but not make us isolated. It is not the hero of solitude, it is a dummy action.

So don't be disturbed easily, I have passed the period of discovering, I need to be nailed down.

Actually I AM wasting time discovering on the last half year. So calm down, calm down. Focused and concentrated.

## Be Humble

I used to be opinionated, which I think is the source of my fault. Actually, I remembered that Huang ever said that: Successful people they don't set up high expectations, or it will reduce their resilience.

Resilience is somehow, a main characteristic to keep committed and forward.
