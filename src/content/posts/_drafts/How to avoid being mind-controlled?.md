## We keep planting our ideas
Commonly, we will state that "in this era of information explosion ... " This is somehow not a wrong thing, but have you ever doubted this statement before fell into someone's logic sandbox?

Maybe (at a high probability) not, at least for me. As I drafting this paper, I mindlessly wrote down this sentence and felt a little weird -- WHY?

In front of this sentence, one may absolutely connect this to Internets, Search engine, Short videos and so on which can stand for the internet revolution. But what if you don't have these things? 

Date this back to ancient ages, there were only books, conversations and governmental messages. Wasn't it an information explosion? Objectively, we and people hundreds of years ago are both human beings, thus the rates of sorting information are basically the same (maybe a little increased, though that's out of our discussion). So what changes is just the platform, the source of information. It is easier and more convenient to catch messages we want.

---
Aha, you are caught by me, I just successfully planted an idea in your mind, and it will be really hard for you to forget. 

So maybe you have understand the meaning of the title, "How to avoid being mind-controlled?", because we are keeping "ideas planted".

Let me take another real life example that confused me for such a long time. It used to be one of my chemical teachers, he said that:" it will be really good if you make your passion into your work." attached with quite a logical reason: 

> Passion provides motivation, which will make your boring work easier to go through.

While it is reasonable, and hard to talk back. But here is the truth (for me), without absolutely strictly math proof, we can't believe everything, even though it is not possible to prove. 

You can't turn that into a mathematical statement, but still, you shouldn't believe it immediately. However, I did, because I could't take counterexamples.

I turned my passion, mathematics, into math contest. Local policy were announced to provide a clear path to attend for university directly. I thought: What A Policy! God helps me!

It keeps long till now, and time went by, until I generally felt bad at math contest. Firstly, I came to realize that math contest here has changed to a different thing from my "ideal" math. But is it the fault of math contest, is it the fault of the policy, or is it my fault? I'm hard to tell. But there was one thing that I could claim: I have forgot my initial mind: where I came from?

Luckily, I met a great teacher: he taught me(also my classmates) in a "mathly" way. Changing vantage points to turn a really hard problem into a trivial one step by step and making the  structure deeper inside clearer.
